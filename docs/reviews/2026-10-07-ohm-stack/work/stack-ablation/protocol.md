## Decisions

Favor deletion when a feature's repeatable whole-workflow value is small relative to its correctness and maintenance surface. A positive point estimate alone does not establish retention. Treat a repeatable multi-second workflow loss or a broad slowdown as a reason to investigate a narrower implementation. Separate noisy/borderline results from established losses, and report uncertainty from the paired observations.

Split the compiler bundle only if removing it causes a substantial repeatable loss. After individual decisions, compare one lean combined stack to the full baseline. Linux results do not establish macOS behavior; platform coverage will be explicit in the final report.
