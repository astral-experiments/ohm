# Proposed disposition of the remaining small patches

These are source recommendations for the frozen 1.99.0-1 stack, not new performance results or approval of a removal build. The source pins are Rust `348339ddae4288ebb3af97d204b19479fef8f5f2`, Cargo `018a2b93409433dc5bc05c5fce838ae69e9bc396`, Clippy `f52143702ff1f265014e87d5e6853e70a3b15728`, and rustfmt `a21f2ab199a5f86ea88535e2a5f888d9e1c67edb`. “Upstream” below means the exact Rust 1.99 tree `b940084d7eb6a299eb4bfeb8e34901bc051e7ac4` and its bundled tools; current upstream development branches were not queried.

Prefer submitting the small general refactors upstream. If upstream does not want them, their historical marginal gains do not justify carrying a separate permanent Ohm layer.

| Rust #100, fast rejection | **Drop the Ohm layout tuning; offer only the argument-identity check upstream.** | The single-argument case and identical-type check already exist in upstream. Ohm adds the generic-argument identity check, splits the many-argument and recursive structural paths into `inline(never)` functions, and adds inlining attributes. ## What the existing ablations cover

The ordinary-semantic arm removes #84/#110/#117, explicitly preserves #85, and leaves #100/#108 and the #104/#112 changes above intact. Its result cannot be credited to removing these patches.

The ordinary const-impl-index arm removes #103 and only #104's dependent `trait_def.rs`/`specialization_graph.rs` consumer. That consumer accounts for +44/−3 of #104's historical +116/−23 production delta; the remaining eight production files are +72/−20. It does not measure the Cow, clone, lang-item, inlining, or normalization changes. The package-only formatter arm retains #17, and the ordinary Clippy traversal arm retains #41. No separate native or latency claim for these small patches follows from those controls.

## Remove empty history, preserve upstream implementations

Twenty-five frozen commits are exactly tree-equal to their parents/bases: ten Rust, eight Cargo, and seven Clippy. Removing these layers during a restack changes no source and needs no runtime benchmark. This is a history recommendation; no live stack, tag, or release manifest was changed here.

- Cargo: #56–60 and #62–64. **#61 is not empty** and must remain a separate decision.
- Clippy: #36/#37/#39/#40/#42/#43/#45. Their preserved PR file arrays are empty and both Git tree identities match.
- Rust: the #44/#49/#50/#51 backports, four later already-upstream read-recorder/MoveData/hygiene cherry-picks, and two superseded Cargo-pin commits. Preserve the actual upstream implementations and the final Cargo pin; remove only the obsolete zero-delta layers.
