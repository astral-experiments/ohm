### Confirmation of the two slower workflows

The two Ruff/ty workflows above were repeated with eight fresh stock/Ohm pairs each on a fresh boot of the same 32-CPU VM. The source revisions, profiles, commands, and CPU checks match the original matrix. This table uses only the new pairs; the original four-run medians remain above. Positive time changes mean Ohm took longer. [Confirmation measurements and provenance](ruff-slower-workflows-1.99.0-27.json)

| Workflow                | Stock Rust |     Ohm | Time change | Slower Ohm pairs |
| ----------------------- | ---------: | ------: | ----------: | ---------------: |
| `edit-test`             |    13.57 s | 14.64 s |      +7.91% |              7/8 |
| `edit-check-test-check` |    20.21 s | 21.76 s |      +7.67% |              8/8 |

The confirmation accepted all 32 planned samples and retained 1 excluded attempt with its reason in the raw evidence.
