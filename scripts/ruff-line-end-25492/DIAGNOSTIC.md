## Build-ID cache control

The first restored-environment worker, run 41464222271, matched every complete ordinary OLD, edited NEW, and empty NEW rustc/Wild argument, inherited environment, selected native object/donor, and typed verdict. Its unchanged visit used a different native-tool namespace and failed. Its samples remain excluded.

Ubuntu perf normally populates its build-ID cache. On this same perf version, adding a disposable 26,936-byte executable kept its checksum, inode and mtime, changed its ctime, and increased its link count from one to two. Cargo #253 includes Unix executable ctime and nanoseconds in the shared native-tool namespace. The repeat uses `perf record --no-buildid-cache` including on the arithmetic calibration. It records the signed and system tool identities immediately before and after each real Cargo process, defers all symbolization until the four ordinary histories and clean typed checks are over, and checks the tool identities again after symbolization. The original four-phase roster, environment and output gates remain mandatory.
