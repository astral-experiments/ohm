# Held-out GNU AArch64 Ruff build with fixed physical compiler runtimes

This registration covers twelve new matched blocks. Earlier native and instruction acquisitions are eligibility controls and contribute zero observations. The rejected first schedule at 289cb599 contributes zero blocks. The public eligibility-only native Actions run 41071713576 has passed all six cross-role and three same-role transitions. Its full archive is pinned in gates.py and its physical procedure is documented in PARKED.md. Each independent block repeats those same ten true Cargo builds before starting fresh target and build stores. The workloads are the first real edit, its immediate unchanged successor, and a separately trained, already-settled build. CPU time and wall time are outside this experiment.

## Estimators and decision

For each of A/M and N/M calculate three ratios of the complete measured guest Ir: first real edit, the sum of that edit plus its immediate repeat, and the separately settled command. Within each job first sum the physical Cargo and all actual compiler counts, then divide the two arms. Report all 12 paired ratios, their median and range, plus the second-through-eleventh order statistics. With 12 independent exchangeable signs the two binomial tails are 13/4096 each. Union bounding across six endpoints gives simultaneous coverage of at least 1 - 6 * 26/4096 = 0.9619140625; report this coverage with its assumptions and do not extrapolate to hardware latency. For each endpoint report the exact one-sided sign probability against ratio 2, counting ties against success. Require all 12 complete native gates and each of all six paired ratios to exceed 2 in all 12 jobs; no preliminary pair is counted. Lower ratios remain reported. Confirmation qualifies an instruction-specific draft only.

Every block retains complete native stdout/stderr, kernel traces, full profile and collector-log bodies, raw ELFs/archives and a SHA-bound terminal for seven days. A separate reader enumerates all successful exec events, checks every per-PID profile against its own native argv, sums own Ir, reopens every archive member/ELF and independently recomputes the endpoints. The operational three-day SDK custody is separate from build-cache cost. On a local storage floor preserve the digest and independently reopened small result for every block, with complete native archives returned to private hosted storage.
